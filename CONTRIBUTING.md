# Contributing

This is an independently maintained, AI-assisted Windows-first prototype. Bug reports, reproducible test cases, documentation improvements and bounded code contributions are welcome. Read the [scope](./docs/01-overview-and-scope.md), [backlog](./docs/10-future-features.md) and [QA/fix workflow](./docs/17-qa-and-fix-readiness.md) first.

## Rights and provenance

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion are under Apache-2.0, as described in section 5 of [LICENSE](./LICENSE). Contributors retain ownership of their contributions; submission is not an assignment of copyright. No separate contributor license agreement is currently required.

Only submit material you have the right to contribute, including any necessary employer/client permission. Identify third-party material, its source and license, and preserve required notices. Disclose material AI assistance and verify the resulting code's behavior and provenance; generated output is not proof of correctness or permission. Do not submit proprietary code, unlicensed assets, credentials, personal data or private footage.

If a contribution is offered on different terms, state that explicitly before submission and obtain maintainer agreement before inclusion.

## Scope and validation

Discuss significant features, dependencies, model integrations, saved-schema changes and packaging work before implementing them. Commercial potential or sponsorship does not automatically expand the roadmap. Prioritize reliable workflows, useful QA evidence and maintainable contributions over feature volume.

Keep changes focused, include regressions for behavior changes and update related documentation. Follow the existing validation commands in the [run guide](./docs/13-run-and-manual-testing.md). State what was tested and what remains unverified; use isolated fixtures rather than user projects. Maintainer review and acceptance are required; no response, merge or delivery deadline is guaranteed.

## Reports and sponsorship

Use synthetic examples and redact tokens, usernames, paths and personal information from reports and logs. Do not publish sensitive reports in public issues.

Sponsorship supports maintenance, testing, documentation and development. Unless separately agreed in writing, it does not purchase ownership, exclusivity, priority, support commitments or guaranteed feature delivery.
